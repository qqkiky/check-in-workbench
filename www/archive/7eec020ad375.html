    The Morning Bull - US Market Morning Update Monday, Aug, 10 2026

    US stock futures are pointing higher this morning, with the S&P 500 contracts up around 0.6%, as investors weigh softer US jobs data against falling bond yields and cooler inflation expectations. July nonfarm payrolls showed a small job loss of 23,000 and weaker wage growth, which signals a slowing job market and less pressure from pay rises. At the same time, the 10 year Treasury yield has dropped to about 4.60% and one year inflation expectations sit at 3.6%, suggesting borrowing costs could ease over time. The big question now is how rate sensitive sectors such as technology, smaller companies and real estate stocks react to this mix of slower growth and gentler inflation.
